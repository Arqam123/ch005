# Course Platform

A marketplace where Students buy and take video courses. This glossary covers the language Students and staff use for getting money back after a purchase.

## Language

### People

**Student**:
A person who buys and takes courses.
_Avoid_: Learner, user (when you mean this person)

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A Student's ask to get their money back for one purchase, along with the reason they give. A purchase can have at most one Refund Request, and it is always in exactly one state: Requested, Approved or Declined.
_Avoid_: Chargeback, Reversal, Cancellation, cancel my purchase, refund (when you mean the ask)

**Requested**:
The state of a Refund Request that has been raised and not yet decided by staff.
_Avoid_: Pending, open, waiting on us, raised

**Approved**:
The state of a Refund Request that staff accepted. It produces a Refund and ends the Student's access to the course.

**Declined**:
The state of a Refund Request that staff turned down. No money is returned, access continues, and the decision is final: no new Refund Request can be raised for that purchase.
_Avoid_: Rejected, denied

**Refund**:
Money actually returned to the Student because a Refund Request was Approved. It always equals the Refundable Amount; there are no partial Refunds.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund Window**:
The 14 days after a purchase during which a Refund Request can be raised.
_Avoid_: Cancel window

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a purchase can have a Refund Request raised right now.
_Avoid_: Cancellation offer

**Refundable Amount**:
The amount a Refund would return. That is the price paid for a purchasing-power-priced purchase, and the course's list price otherwise.

### Outside the platform

**Chargeback**:
A dispute the cardholder opens with their bank or card issuer, outside the platform. It is not something a Student asks us for, and it is never a Refund Request. Not modelled yet.
